May you have a relatively "peaceful easy feeling" today,
Evermore peaceful as an abiding love comes to live in your heart.
Tear of the drapes I say and jump out into the fray,
As your sister ties her shoelaces before she makes another start.

You see, a parting between loved ones is needed before
They can come back to life and be intimate again with it's secrets.
She's strong enough to soon be back on love's threshing floor,
So don't over fret, rather lead the family in remembrance not mourning. Let's

Dance in her memory, though we may feel the dregs of what life seems to be offering today.
Remember to celebrate a life well spent, though painful the end.
There's a lot of things this Bard, to each of you would like to say.
Of what little we know of the afterlife where, our loving family we do send.
