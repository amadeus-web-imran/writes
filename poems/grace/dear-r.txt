14 years ago I felt elated to be at the engagement
Of your parents. So many good things I dreamed of,
That their relationship would blossom, not torment
Your father a long long time. At the wedding I spoke of

Them living a full life, with you their anchor, you see
To many, life is a blessing and we feel gratitude.
Yet even to those upset or confused, it can be
A place of learning, where we learn to not be crude,

But rather sensitive to the plight of the average soul.
I've wished greatly to meet you, since even before fate
Drove them apart, now I wish only for you to feel whole,
Not shattered as speaks this man in black. It's not late

For you to find healing - our world is undergoing a
Metamorphosis and it's the sensitive ones who can
Stand up for their beliefs, help wage wars where a
Small win daily can turn the wave of hate into an

Expression of love. Deep thoughts from a wellwisher;
You may divine their meaning in the fullness of time.
For now don't feel overburdened, just feel the cheer
As you go make yours and our lives feel sublime.
