Face to face, heart to heart, body to body - sang Alabama,
Dan Seals it was spoke of a couple as raged on.
This poem is about humanity - heart to heart, sweet Mama.
While the Devil's hordes RAZE ON. And here we groan,

Seemingly bereft of our senses as city here falleth,
And lawmaker there quits. 8 BILLION souls cry to you
Liberty, for we're tired of all the wanton killing. So cometh
To us soon, that in your wake, we may find the courage to not be blue.

But how can YOU take the first step when we are indeed the authors of OUR destiny?
You serve as a cosmic force like the Sun - whose light we can focus and turn,
Until it turns aflame that which we turned it's might to. Now hear Gaia's DECREE,
That liberty is not some plaything to arrive at our snap of finger, rather we must BURN

Our every impurity, be honest, while doing the Lord's work - in whatsoever manner
We see fit. The Hour of God may be upon us, but not to those with sealed ears,
Who hear not the summons. O' HORDES, play not with Nature or Humanity - your spanner
Is no longer wanted here on Earth, so trip us NO MORE. Wake up me lads n lassies, leave off the fears!

Those cities never truly fell, for those ascended were already atop Jacob's Ladder
And those as didn't quite make it were yet gathered as unto the winepress of God.
The lawmaker's visage yet lingers, for in him may hang the grit of HIS entire cadre.
So learn to burn dear friends, let Liberty walk again among us - and NOONE find that odd.
